# Hardware and Printers

## Laptop screen flickering
1. Update the graphics driver from the software portal.
2. Lower the refresh rate in display settings.
3. Connect an external monitor. If it looks fine, the laptop panel or cable is faulty and needs a repair.

## Laptop will not turn on
1. Plug in the charger and wait 15 minutes.
2. Hold the power button for 30 seconds to drain residual power, then press it once.
3. Try a different power outlet and charger if available.
If it still does not start, the helpdesk will arrange a replacement device.

## Printer paper jam or blank pages
1. Turn the printer off and open all access doors.
2. Gently pull jammed paper in the direction of the paper path.
3. Replace the toner cartridge if pages print faint or blank.
4. Contact the helpdesk with the printer label ID if the error returns.

## Keyboard, mouse and headset requests
Peripherals can be requested through the helpdesk. Mention your desk location and the model you use now. Standard items ship within two working days.
