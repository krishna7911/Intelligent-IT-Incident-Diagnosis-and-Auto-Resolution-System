# Accounts, Passwords and MFA

## Reset a forgotten password
1. Open the company sign-in page and choose "Forgot password".
2. Enter your work email and complete the verification code.
3. Create a new password with at least 12 characters, one number and one symbol.
4. Sign out of all devices, then sign in again with the new password.
If the reset email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder or ask the helpdesk to trigger a manual reset.

## Account locked after failed attempts
Accounts lock for 30 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
1. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or request an unlock from the helpdesk.
2. Make sure caps lock is off and you are using the new password if it was changed recently.
3. Update saved passwords on your phone and laptop so they stop retrying with the old one.

## Move multi-factor authentication to a new phone
1. Keep your old phone until the move is finished.
2. Open the security settings page and choose "Add authenticator device".
3. Scan the QR code with the authenticator app on the new phone.
4. Remove the old device after you confirm the new one works.
If the old phone is lost, contact the helpdesk to verify your identity and reset MFA.

## Request access to a shared folder or system
Access requests need manager approval. Include the system name, the folder path and the business reason in your ticket.
