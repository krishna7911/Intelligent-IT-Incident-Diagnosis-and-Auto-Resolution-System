# Security and Phishing

## Reporting a phishing email
1. Do not click links or open attachments.
2. Use the Report Phishing button in Outlook, or forward the email as an attachment to the security team.
3. Delete the message afterwards.
4. If you clicked a link or entered your password, change your password immediately and tell the helpdesk. Mark the ticket as urgent.

## Suspected malware or virus
1. Disconnect the device from WiFi and unplug the network cable.
2. Do not restart or delete files.
3. Contact the helpdesk right away so the security team can isolate and scan the machine.

## Lost or stolen device
Report it immediately with the time and place it was lost. The security team will lock the device remotely and revoke your sessions.

## Unknown login alert
If you see a sign-in from a country you have not visited, change your password, remove unknown devices from your account and report it as a possible breach.
